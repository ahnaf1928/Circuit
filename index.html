Step 01 — একদম Zero থেকে Basics

1. Polyphase System কী?

Exam Definition (2 marks):

A polyphase system is a system in which AC sources operate at the same frequency but have different phase angles.

সহজভাবে, একাধিক AC voltage একই frequency-তে কাজ করছে, কিন্তু তাদের phase angle আলাদা—এটাই Polyphase System।

এর মধ্যে Three-phase System সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

2. Three-phase System কী?

একটি Three-phase System-এ তিনটি AC voltage থাকে, যাদের মধ্যে phase difference 120°।

ধরো, তিনটি phase হলো a, b, c।

Positive phase sequence (abc / RYB)

Three voltages of equal magnitude, separated by 120°.

Why is Three-phase System important? (4 marks)

ম্যামের L-01, page 6-এর চারটি point:

  1. Most electrical power is generated and distributed using three-phase systems.
  2. Total instantaneous power can be constant.
  3. It requires less conductor material than an equivalent single-phase system for the same power.
  4. Three-phase motors are generally self-starting.

এই চারটি point পরীক্ষায় সরাসরি লিখতে পারবে।

3. Balanced Three-phase System

Definition:

A balanced three-phase system has equal source voltage magnitudes separated by 120°, with equal load impedances in all three phases.

অর্থাৎ:

\[|V_{an}|=|V_{bn}|=|V_{cn}|\]
\[Z_a=Z_b=Z_c\]

এখানে \(Z\) = Impedance, যার unit Ω (ohm)।

Step 02 — Phase Sequence

এটা খুবই গুরুত্বপূর্ণ। তোমার দুইটা lecture-এই আছে।

1. Phase Sequence কী?

Exam Definition:

Phase sequence is the time order in which the phase voltages reach their maximum values.

দুই ধরনের Phase Sequence আছে:

Positive Sequence Negative Sequence
abc / RYB acb / RBY
a → b → c a → c → b
b lags a by 120° b leads a by 120°

Positive (abc) Phasor

যদি phase voltage 230 V হয়:

\[V_{an}=230\angle0^\circ\]
\[V_{bn}=230\angle-120^\circ\]
\[V_{cn}=230\angle+120^\circ\]

Negative (acb) Phasor

\[V_{an}=230\angle0^\circ\]
\[V_{bn}=230\angle+120^\circ\]
\[V_{cn}=230\angle-120^\circ\]

মনে রাখবে: Positive-এ b হলো −120°, Negative-এ b হলো +120°, যখন a-এর angle 0° ধরা হয়।

2. Sinusoidal Expression

L-01, page 8 অনুযায়ী, positive sequence-এর জন্য:

\[v_{an}=V_m\sin(\omega t)\]
\[v_{bn}=V_m\sin(\omega t-120^\circ)\]
\[v_{cn}=V_m\sin(\omega t+120^\circ)\]

এখানে:

  • \(V_m\) = Maximum/peak voltage
  • \(\omega=2\pi f\) = Angular frequency
  • \(f\) = Frequency

50 Hz হলে:

\[\omega=2\pi(50)=100\pi\\;\text{rad/s}\]

RMS এবং Peak voltage-এর সম্পর্ক:

\[\boxed{V_{rms}=\frac{V_m}{\sqrt3}\\;\text{নয়}}\]

সঠিক সম্পর্ক:

\[\boxed{V_{rms}=\frac{V_m}{\sqrt2}}\]

এখানে অনেকেই √2 আর √3 গুলিয়ে ফেলে। √2 ব্যবহার করবে peak-to-RMS conversion-এর জন্য; √3 ব্যবহার করবে balanced Y-এর line-to-phase voltage conversion-এর জন্য।

3. Phase Sequence বের করার নিয়ম

L-01-এর page 10–12 অনুযায়ী:

  1. Voltage expressions-গুলোকে phasor form-এ convert করবে।
  2. Phasor diagram-এ একটি fixed reference point ধরবে।
  3. Phasors counterclockwise rotate করলে কোনটি আগে fixed point অতিক্রম করে তা দেখবে।

Special note: L-02 page 3-এ \(-200\cos(\omega t)\)-এর sine conversion-এ একটি sign inconsistency আছে। সঠিক identity হলো:

\[-200\cos(\omega t)=200\sin(\omega t-90^\circ)\]

তাই ওই example-এর conversion অন্ধভাবে মুখস্থ করবে না।

Step 03 — Balanced Y-Y Connection

MOST IMPORTANT NUMERICAL TOPIC

এবার আসি সেই অংশে যেটা PYQ-তে সরাসরি আছে।

1. Y-Y Connection মানে কী?

Y-Y means both the three-phase source and the three-phase load are star (Y) connected.

এতে দুটি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ relation:

Line Voltage

\(V_L=\sqrt3 V_P\)

Line Current

\(I_L=I_P\)

For positive sequence, line voltage leads its corresponding phase voltage by 30°.

2. Line Voltage আর Phase Voltage-এর পার্থক্য

Phase Voltage Line Voltage
একটি line এবং neutral-এর মধ্যে দুটি line-এর মধ্যে
\(V_{an},V_{bn},V_{cn}\) \(V_{ab},V_{bc},V_{ca}\)
Example: 230 V Example: 398.37 V

3. Derivation: Prove \(V_L=\sqrt3 V_P\)

এটা L-02, pages 8–10-এর গুরুত্বপূর্ণ derivation।

Given: Balanced Y-Y system with positive abc sequence.

Applying KVL:

\[-V_{an}+V_{ab}+V_{bn}=0\]

Therefore,

\[V_{ab}=V_{an}-V_{bn}\]
\[V_{ab}=V_{an}+V_{nb}\]

The angle between \(V_{an}\) and \(V_{nb}\) is 60°.

Using vector addition:

\[V_L=\sqrt{V_P^2+V_P^2+2V_P^2\cos60^\circ}\]

Since \(\cos60^\circ=\frac12\),

\[V_L=\sqrt{V_P^2+V_P^2+V_P^2}\]
\[\boxed{V_L=\sqrt3 V_P}\]

For positive sequence, the resulting line-voltage phasor leads the corresponding phase-voltage phasor by 30°.

Final conclusion (exam-এ অবশ্যই লিখবে):

In a balanced Y-Y system, the magnitude of line voltage is √3 times the phase voltage, and the line voltage leads its corresponding phase voltage by 30° for positive sequence.

For negative sequence, the line voltage lags by 30°.

Step 04 — PYQ Numerical: Complete Solution

এবার তোমার দেওয়া আগের প্রশ্নের Y-connected অংশ solve করি।

Given:

\[V_{ph}=230\\;V\]
\[Z_{ph}=20\angle30^\circ\\;\Omega\]

Frequency = 50 Hz

Assume positive (abc) sequence.

(a) Determine the line-to-line voltages

প্রথমে:

\[V_L=\sqrt3\times230\]
\[V_L=398.37\\;V\]

তাহলে:

\[\boxed{V_{ab}=398.37\angle30^\circ\\;V}\]
\[\boxed{V_{bc}=398.37\angle-90^\circ\\;V}\]
\[\boxed{V_{ca}=398.37\angle150^\circ\\;V}\]

এখানে \(150^\circ\) এবং \(-210^\circ\) একই phasor direction নির্দেশ করে।

Phasor diagram আঁকার সময় phase voltage-এর angle 0°, −120°, +120° এবং line voltage-এর angle +30°, −90°, +150° দেখাবে।

(b-i) Calculate the phase currents

Ohm's law:

\[I_{ph}=\frac{V_{ph}}{Z_{ph}}\]

প্রথম phase:

\[I_a=\frac{230\angle0^\circ}{20\angle30^\circ}\]

Magnitude divide এবং angle subtract করবে।

\[\boxed{I_a=11.5\angle-30^\circ\\;A}\]

একইভাবে:

\[I_b=\frac{230\angle-120^\circ}{20\angle30^\circ}\]
\[\boxed{I_b=11.5\angle-150^\circ\\;A}\]
\[I_c=\frac{230\angle120^\circ}{20\angle30^\circ}\]
\[\boxed{I_c=11.5\angle90^\circ\\;A}\]

Shortcut: Impedance angle +30° হওয়ায় প্রতিটি current নিজ নিজ phase voltage থেকে 30° lag করছে।

(b-ii) Determine the line currents

For Y connection:

\[I_L=I_{ph}\]

সুতরাং:

\[\boxed{I_A=11.5\angle-30^\circ A}\]
\[\boxed{I_B=11.5\angle-150^\circ A}\]
\[\boxed{I_C=11.5\angle90^\circ A}\]

এখানে √3 দিয়ে current multiply করবে না।

(b-iii) Find the total consumed power

এই formula-টি PYQ-এর power অংশ solve করার জন্য standard balanced three-phase power relation; তোমার uploaded slides-এ power derivation আলাদাভাবে দেখানো হয়নি।

\[P=3V_{ph}I_{ph}\cos\phi\]

Here,

\[\phi=30^\circ\]
\[P=3\times230\times11.5\times\cos30^\circ\]
\[P=3\times230\times11.5\times0.866\]
\[\boxed{P\approx6872\\;W=6.87\\;kW}\]

Solved PYQ — Final Answers

Quantity Answer
Line voltage 398.37 V
Phase current magnitude 11.5 A
Line current magnitude 11.5 A
Power factor 0.866 lagging
Total real power 6.87 kW

এই numerical-এর (a) এবং (b) অংশ আগের CT-এর 12 marks বহন করেছিল। এই pattern-এর প্রশ্ন দ্রুত solve করতে পারা তোমার preparation-এর বড় অংশ।

Step 05 — আরও দুটি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ Proof

1. Prove that phasor summation of balanced three-phase voltages is zero

For positive sequence:

\[V_{an}=V_P\angle0^\circ\]
\[V_{bn}=V_P\angle-120^\circ\]
\[V_{cn}=V_P\angle120^\circ\]

In rectangular form:

\[V_{an}=V_P\]
\[V_{bn}=-\frac{V_P}{2}-j\frac{\sqrt3V_P}{2}\]
\[V_{cn}=-\frac{V_P}{2}+j\frac{\sqrt3V_P}{2}\]

Adding:

\[V_{an}+V_{bn}+V_{cn} =V_P-\frac{V_P}{2}-\frac{V_P}{2}\]

The imaginary components also cancel.

\[\boxed{V_{an}+V_{bn}+V_{cn}=0}\]

Similarly, for balanced phase currents:

\[\boxed{I_a+I_b+I_c=0}\]

এই proof-টা L-02-এর pages 4–5-এর একই conclusion প্রতিষ্ঠা করে।

2. Contribution of Neutral Wire in Y-Y System

ম্যামের L-02-এর pages 13–15 থেকে এটি পড়বে।

For a balanced Y-Y system, considering equal per-phase total impedance \(Z_Y\):

\[V_{an}=I_aZ_Y+V_{Nn}\]
\[V_{bn}=I_bZ_Y+V_{Nn}\]
\[V_{cn}=I_cZ_Y+V_{Nn}\]

Adding all three equations:

\[V_{an}+V_{bn}+V_{cn} =(I_a+I_b+I_c)Z_Y+3V_{Nn}\]

For a balanced system:

\[0=0+3V_{Nn}\]

Therefore,

\[\boxed{V_{Nn}=0}\]

And,

\[\boxed{I_n=I_a+I_b+I_c=0}\]

Important conclusion: In an ideal balanced Y-Y system, the neutral current is zero. However, the neutral wire is useful in practical systems because loads may become unbalanced.

Question 1 — 4 Marks

Define a balanced three-phase system. State two advantages of a three-phase system.

Exam-ready Answer

Definition of Balanced Three-phase System

A balanced three-phase system is a system in which the three-phase source voltages have equal magnitudes and are displaced by 120° from one another, while the load impedances of all three phases are equal.

Mathematically,

\[|V_{an}|=|V_{bn}|=|V_{cn}|=V_P\]

and

\[Z_a=Z_b=Z_c=Z\]

Advantages of Three-phase Systems

  1. Economical Power Transmission: A three-phase system requires less conductor material than an equivalent single-phase system for transmitting the same amount of power.

  2. Constant Power: The total instantaneous power delivered to a balanced three-phase load is constant, rather than pulsating.

বাংলায় বুঝে নাও

ধরো, তিনটি phase আছে: a, b, c।

প্রতিটা phase যদি 230 V supply দেয় এবং তাদের phase difference 120° হয়, তাহলে source voltages balanced।

এখন তিনটি phase-এ যদি সমান impedance থাকে, যেমন:

\[Z_a=Z_b=Z_c=10\\;\Omega\]

তাহলে পুরো system balanced three-phase system।

মনে রাখবে:

  • Voltage magnitude সমান।
  • Phase difference 120°।
  • Load impedance সমান।

Marks distribution (suggested): Definition 2 + two advantages 2 = 4 marks।

এই সুবিধাগুলো তোমার L-01-এর page 6-এ আছে।

Question 2 — 4 Marks

Define phase sequence. Write the phasor expressions for both positive and negative sequence when the phase voltage is 100 V.

Exam-ready Answer

Definition of Phase Sequence

Phase sequence is the time order in which the phase voltages of a three-phase system attain their maximum values.

There are two types of phase sequences:

  1. Positive phase sequence (abc or RYB).
  2. Negative phase sequence (acb or RBY).

(a) Positive Phase Sequence (abc)

Taking phase a as the reference and assuming 100 V RMS phase voltage:

\[\boxed{V_{an}=100\angle0^\circ\\;V}\]
\[\boxed{V_{bn}=100\angle-120^\circ\\;V}\]
\[\boxed{V_{cn}=100\angle+120^\circ\\;V}\]

The phases attain their maximum values in the order a → b → c.

(b) Negative Phase Sequence (acb)

\[\boxed{V_{an}=100\angle0^\circ\\;V}\]
\[\boxed{V_{bn}=100\angle+120^\circ\\;V}\]
\[\boxed{V_{cn}=100\angle-120^\circ\\;V}\]

The phases attain their maximum values in the order a → c → b.

Phasor Diagram

Positive (abc)

b = −120°, c = +120°

Negative (acb)

b = +120°, c = −120°

বাংলায় ব্যাখ্যা

তিনটি voltage একই সময়ে সর্বোচ্চ মানে পৌঁছায় না। একটি maximum-এ পৌঁছানোর পর অন্যটি পৌঁছায়। এই ক্রমটাই Phase Sequence।

Positive sequence হলে:

  • a-এর angle 0°
  • b-এর angle −120°
  • c-এর angle +120°

Negative sequence হলে b ও c-এর angle উল্টে যাবে।

এখানে সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ: 100 V হলো প্রতিটি phase-এর voltage magnitude। তাই 120° দিয়ে voltage গুণ বা ভাগ করবে না। শুধু angle পরিবর্তন হবে।

Extra: Sinusoidal Expression

যেহেতু L-01-এ sinusoidal representation-ও আছে, তাই এটাও শিখে রাখবে।

100 V RMS হলে:

\[V_m=100\sqrt2=141.42\\;V\]

Positive sequence:

\[v_{an}=141.42\sin(\omega t)\]
\[v_{bn}=141.42\sin(\omega t-120^\circ)\]
\[v_{cn}=141.42\sin(\omega t+120^\circ)\]

Negative sequence:

\[v_{an}=141.42\sin(\omega t)\]
\[v_{bn}=141.42\sin(\omega t+120^\circ)\]
\[v_{cn}=141.42\sin(\omega t-120^\circ)\]

Note: Phasor expression-এ RMS magnitude লিখেছি। Time-domain sine expression-এ peak magnitude ব্যবহার করেছি।

Question 3 — 4 Marks

Derive the relation between line voltage and phase voltage for a balanced Y-Y system of positive sequence.

Source: L-02, pages 7–10

এই derivation-টা খুব ভালোভাবে বুঝবে। কারণ 4 marks-এর theory হিসেবেও আসতে পারে, আবার numerical-এর প্রথম step-এও লাগবে।

Step 1: Given Conditions

Consider a balanced Y-Y connected three-phase system having positive (abc) phase sequence.

Let,

\[V_P=\text{Phase voltage magnitude}\]
\[V_L=\text{Line voltage magnitude}\]

The phase voltages are:

\[V_{an}=V_P\angle0^\circ\]
\[V_{bn}=V_P\angle-120^\circ\]
\[V_{cn}=V_P\angle120^\circ\]

Step 2: Apply Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

Applying KVL to the loop containing \(V_{an},V_{ab}\) and \(V_{bn}\):

\[-V_{an}+V_{ab}+V_{bn}=0\]

Rearranging,

\[\boxed{V_{ab}=V_{an}-V_{bn}}\]

Since,

\[-V_{bn}=V_{nb}\]

Therefore,

\[\boxed{V_{ab}=V_{an}+V_{nb}}\]

Step 3: Apply Vector Addition

The angle between \(V_{an}\) and \(V_{nb}\) is 60°.

Using the law of cosines:

\[V_{ab}=\sqrt{V_{an}^2+V_{nb}^2+2V_{an}V_{nb}\cos60^\circ}\]

For a balanced system,

\[|V_{an}|=|V_{nb}|=V_P\]

Hence,

\[V_L=\sqrt{V_P^2+V_P^2+2V_P^2\cos60^\circ}\]

We know,

\[\cos60^\circ=\frac12\]

Putting the value:

\[V_L=\sqrt{V_P^2+V_P^2+2V_P^2\times\frac12}\]
\[V_L=\sqrt{3V_P^2}\]

Therefore,

\[\boxed{V_L=\sqrt3V_P}\]

Step 4: Determine the Phase Angle

Because \(V_{an}\) and \(V_{nb}\) have equal magnitudes, their resultant bisects the 60° angle.

Therefore,

\[\theta=\frac{60^\circ}{2}=30^\circ\]

So,

\[\boxed{V_{ab}=\sqrt3V_P\angle30^\circ}\]

Similarly,

\[\boxed{V_{bc}=\sqrt3V_P\angle-90^\circ}\]
\[\boxed{V_{ca}=\sqrt3V_P\angle150^\circ}\]

Final Conclusion

For a balanced Y-Y system with positive phase sequence:

\[\boxed{V_L=\sqrt3V_P}\]
\[\boxed{I_L=I_P}\]

The line voltage is √3 times the phase voltage in magnitude and leads the corresponding phase voltage by 30°.

বাংলায় একদম সহজ ব্যাখ্যা

ধরো, দুইটা phasor আছে:

  • \(V_{an}\) = 100 V
  • \(V_{nb}\) = 100 V

এদের মাঝের angle = 60°।

এখন আমরা দুটো vector যোগ করছি। Vector যোগ করলে magnitude সবসময় সাধারণ যোগফলের মতো হয় না। Angle বিবেচনা করতে হয়।

তাই আমরা cosine rule ব্যবহার করেছি।

আর যেহেতু দুইটা vector-এর magnitude সমান, resultant তাদের মধ্যবর্তী 60° angle-কে সমান দুই ভাগ করে।

তাই resultant-এর angle:

\[60^\circ\div2=30^\circ\]

এজন্য positive sequence-এ line voltage 30° lead করে।

Exam Tip: KVL equation, vector addition formula, √3 result এবং 30° conclusion—এই চারটি অংশ অবশ্যই দেখাবে।

Question 4 — 6 Marks

A balanced Y-Y system has a phase voltage of 200 V and load impedance of \(10\angle30^\circ\\;\Omega\) per phase. Calculate all three phase currents, line currents and total real power. Assume abc sequence.

এটা সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ numerical practice। এখন একদম ধাপে ধাপে করি।

Step 1: Write Given Data

Given,

Phase voltage:

\[V_P=200\\;V\]

Load impedance:

\[Z_P=10\angle30^\circ\\;\Omega\]

Phase sequence = abc (positive).

We have to determine:

  1. Phase currents \(I_a,I_b,I_c\)
  2. Line currents \(I_A,I_B,I_C\)
  3. Total real power \(P\)

Step 2: Determine Phase Voltages

For positive sequence:

\[V_{an}=V_P\angle0^\circ\]
\[V_{bn}=V_P\angle-120^\circ\]
\[V_{cn}=V_P\angle120^\circ\]

Since \(V_P=200\\;V\),

\[\boxed{V_{an}=200\angle0^\circ\\;V}\]
\[\boxed{V_{bn}=200\angle-120^\circ\\;V}\]
\[\boxed{V_{cn}=200\angle120^\circ\\;V}\]

Step 3: Calculate Phase Currents

Using Ohm's Law:

\[I=\frac{V}{Z}\]

মনে রাখবে, polar form-এ divide করলে:

\[\frac{A\angle\alpha}{B\angle\beta} =\frac AB\angle(\alpha-\beta)\]

অর্থাৎ magnitude ভাগ এবং angle বিয়োগ।

Phase a Current

\[I_a=\frac{V_{an}}{Z_P}\]
\[I_a=\frac{200\angle0^\circ}{10\angle30^\circ}\]
\[I_a=\frac{200}{10}\angle(0^\circ-30^\circ)\]
\[\boxed{I_a=20\angle-30^\circ\\;A}\]

Phase b Current

\[I_b=\frac{V_{bn}}{Z_P}\]
\[I_b=\frac{200\angle-120^\circ}{10\angle30^\circ}\]
\[I_b=20\angle(-120^\circ-30^\circ)\]
\[\boxed{I_b=20\angle-150^\circ\\;A}\]

Phase c Current

\[I_c=\frac{V_{cn}}{Z_P}\]
\[I_c=\frac{200\angle120^\circ}{10\angle30^\circ}\]
\[I_c=20\angle(120^\circ-30^\circ)\]
\[\boxed{I_c=20\angle90^\circ\\;A}\]

Phase Current Summary

Phase Phase Voltage Phase Current
a \(200\angle0^\circ\) V \(20\angle-30^\circ\) A
b \(200\angle-120^\circ\) V \(20\angle-150^\circ\) A
c \(200\angle120^\circ\) V \(20\angle90^\circ\) A

বাংলায় বুঝো: প্রত্যেক phase-এর voltage আলাদা angle-এ আছে। কিন্তু impedance তিন phase-এই সমান। তাই current magnitude একই 20 A, শুধু angle আলাদা।

Step 4: Calculate Line Currents

For a balanced Y-connected load:

\[\boxed{I_L=I_P}\]

Therefore,

\[\boxed{I_A=I_a=20\angle-30^\circ\\;A}\]
\[\boxed{I_B=I_b=20\angle-150^\circ\\;A}\]
\[\boxed{I_C=I_c=20\angle90^\circ\\;A}\]

Hence, the magnitude of each line current is:

\[\boxed{I_L=20\\;A}\]

Y connection-এ line current এবং phase current সমান।

Step 5: Calculate Total Real Power

For a balanced three-phase system:

\[\boxed{P=3V_PI_P\cos\phi}\]

Here,

\[V_P=200\\;V\]
\[I_P=20\\;A\]

Impedance angle,

\[\phi=30^\circ\]

Therefore,

\[P=3\times200\times20\times\cos30^\circ\]

We know,

\[\cos30^\circ=0.8660\]

So,

\[P=3\times200\times20\times0.8660\]
\[P=12000\times0.8660\]
\[P=10392.3\\;W\]

Therefore,

\[\boxed{P=10.39\\;kW}\]

Since impedance angle is positive, the current lags the voltage.

Thus, power factor:

\[\boxed{PF=0.866\text{ lagging}}\]

Question 5 — 2 Marks

What is the neutral current in an ideal balanced Y-Y system? Explain briefly.

Exam-ready Answer

In an ideal balanced Y-Y system, the neutral current is zero because the three-phase currents have equal magnitudes and are displaced by 120° from one another.

By Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL), the neutral current is the phasor sum of three phase currents:

\[I_n=I_a+I_b+I_c\]

For a balanced system:

\[I_a+I_b+I_c=0\]

Therefore,

\[\boxed{I_n=0}\]

Conclusion: The neutral wire carries no current under ideal balanced loading conditions. However, it carries the resultant unbalanced current when the loads are unequal.

বাংলায় ব্যাখ্যা

ধরো, তিনটি phase-এর current:

\[I_a=10\angle0^\circ A\]
\[I_b=10\angle-120^\circ A\]
\[I_c=10\angle120^\circ A\]

এদের magnitude সমান হলেও direction আলাদা।

তাই তিনটি current-এর phasor যোগফল:

\[I_a+I_b+I_c=0\]

অর্থাৎ Neutral Current = 0 A।
