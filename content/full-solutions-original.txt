# Question 1 — 4 Marks

Define a balanced three-phase system. State two advantages of a three-phase system.

## Exam-ready Answer

### Definition of Balanced Three-phase System

A balanced three-phase system is a system in which the three-phase source voltages have equal magnitudes and are displaced by 120° from one another, while the load impedances of all three phases are equal.

Mathematically,

\\[ |V\_{an}|=|V\_{bn}|=|V\_{cn}|=V_P \\]

and

\\[ Z_a=Z_b=Z_c=Z \\]

### Advantages of Three-phase Systems

1\. Economical Power Transmission: A three-phase system requires less conductor material than an equivalent single-phase system for transmitting the same amount of power.

2\. Constant Power: The total instantaneous power delivered to a balanced three-phase load is constant, rather than pulsating.

## বাংলায় বুঝে নাও

ধরো, তিনটি phase আছে: a, b, c।

প্রতিটা phase যদি 230 V supply দেয় এবং তাদের phase difference 120° হয়, তাহলে source voltages balanced।

এখন তিনটি phase-এ যদি সমান impedance থাকে, যেমন:

\\[ Z_a=Z_b=Z_c=10\\;\Omega \\]

তাহলে পুরো system balanced three-phase system।

মনে রাখবে:

- Voltage magnitude সমান।
- Phase difference 120°।
- Load impedance সমান।

Marks distribution (suggested): Definition 2 + two advantages 2 = 4 marks।

# Question 2 — 4 Marks

Define phase sequence. Write the phasor expressions for both positive and negative sequence when the phase voltage is 100 V.

## Exam-ready Answer

### Definition of Phase Sequence

Phase sequence is the time order in which the phase voltages of a three-phase system attain their maximum values.

There are two types of phase sequences:

1. Positive phase sequence (abc or RYB).
2. Negative phase sequence (acb or RBY).

### (a) Positive Phase Sequence (abc)

Taking phase a as the reference and assuming 100 V RMS phase voltage:

\\[ \boxed{V\_{an}=100\angle0^\circ\\;V} \\]

\\[ \boxed{V\_{bn}=100\angle-120^\circ\\;V} \\]

\\[ \boxed{V\_{cn}=100\angle+120^\circ\\;V} \\]

The phases attain their maximum values in the order a → b → c.

### (b) Negative Phase Sequence (acb)

\\[ \boxed{V\_{an}=100\angle0^\circ\\;V} \\]

\\[ \boxed{V\_{bn}=100\angle+120^\circ\\;V} \\]

\\[ \boxed{V\_{cn}=100\angle-120^\circ\\;V} \\]

The phases attain their maximum values in the order a → c → b.

## বাংলায় ব্যাখ্যা

তিনটি voltage একই সময়ে সর্বোচ্চ মানে পৌঁছায় না। একটি maximum-এ পৌঁছানোর পর অন্যটি পৌঁছায়। এই ক্রমটাই Phase Sequence।

Positive sequence হলে:

- a-এর angle 0°
- b-এর angle −120°
- c-এর angle +120°

Negative sequence হলে b ও c-এর angle উল্টে যাবে।

এখানে সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ: 100 V হলো প্রতিটি phase-এর voltage magnitude। তাই 120° দিয়ে voltage গুণ বা ভাগ করবে না। শুধু angle পরিবর্তন হবে।

### Extra: Sinusoidal Expression

যেহেতু L-01-এ sinusoidal representation-ও আছে, তাই এটাও শিখে রাখবে।

100 V RMS হলে:

\\[ V_m=100\sqrt2=141.42\\;V \\]

Positive sequence:

\\[ v\_{an}=141.42\sin(\omega t) \\]

\\[ v\_{bn}=141.42\sin(\omega t-120^\circ) \\]

\\[ v\_{cn}=141.42\sin(\omega t+120^\circ) \\]

Negative sequence:

\\[ v\_{an}=141.42\sin(\omega t) \\]

\\[ v\_{bn}=141.42\sin(\omega t+120^\circ) \\]

\\[ v\_{cn}=141.42\sin(\omega t-120^\circ) \\]

Note: Phasor expression-এ RMS magnitude লিখেছি। Time-domain sine expression-এ peak magnitude ব্যবহার করেছি।

# Question 3 — 4 Marks

Derive the relation between line voltage and phase voltage for a balanced Y-Y system of positive sequence.

Source: L-02, pages 7–10

এই derivation-টা খুব ভালোভাবে বুঝবে। কারণ 4 marks-এর theory হিসেবেও আসতে পারে, আবার numerical-এর প্রথম step-এও লাগবে।

## Step 1: Given Conditions

Consider a balanced Y-Y connected three-phase system having positive (abc) phase sequence.

Let,

\\[ V_P=\text{Phase voltage magnitude} \\]

\\[ V_L=\text{Line voltage magnitude} \\]

The phase voltages are:

\\[ V\_{an}=V_P\angle0^\circ \\]

\\[ V\_{bn}=V_P\angle-120^\circ \\]

\\[ V\_{cn}=V_P\angle120^\circ \\]

## Step 2: Apply Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

Applying KVL to the loop containing \\(V\_{an},V\_{ab}\\) and \\(V\_{bn}\\):

\\[ -V\_{an}+V\_{ab}+V\_{bn}=0 \\]

Rearranging,

\\[ \boxed{V\_{ab}=V\_{an}-V\_{bn}} \\]

Since,

\\[ -V\_{bn}=V\_{nb} \\]

Therefore,

\\[ \boxed{V\_{ab}=V\_{an}+V\_{nb}} \\]

## Step 3: Apply Vector Addition

The angle between \\(V\_{an}\\) and \\(V\_{nb}\\) is 60°.

Using the law of cosines:

\\[ V\_{ab}=\sqrt{V\_{an}^2+V\_{nb}^2+2V\_{an}V\_{nb}\cos60^\circ} \\]

For a balanced system,

\\[ |V\_{an}|=|V\_{nb}|=V_P \\]

Hence,

\\[ V_L=\sqrt{V_P^2+V_P^2+2V_P^2\cos60^\circ} \\]

We know,

\\[ \cos60^\circ=\frac12 \\]

Putting the value:

\\[ V_L=\sqrt{V_P^2+V_P^2+2V_P^2\times\frac12} \\]

\\[ V_L=\sqrt{3V_P^2} \\]

Therefore,

\\[ \boxed{V_L=\sqrt3V_P} \\]

## Step 4: Determine the Phase Angle

Because \\(V\_{an}\\) and \\(V\_{nb}\\) have equal magnitudes, their resultant bisects the 60° angle.

Therefore,

\\[ \theta=\frac{60^\circ}{2}=30^\circ \\]

So,

\\[ \boxed{V\_{ab}=\sqrt3V_P\angle30^\circ} \\]

Similarly,

\\[ \boxed{V\_{bc}=\sqrt3V_P\angle-90^\circ} \\]

\\[ \boxed{V\_{ca}=\sqrt3V_P\angle150^\circ} \\]

## Final Conclusion

For a balanced Y-Y system with positive phase sequence:

\\[ \boxed{V_L=\sqrt3V_P} \\]

\\[ \boxed{I_L=I_P} \\]

The line voltage is √3 times the phase voltage in magnitude and leads the corresponding phase voltage by 30°.

## বাংলায় একদম সহজ ব্যাখ্যা

ধরো, দুইটা phasor আছে:

- \\(V\_{an}\\) = 100 V
- \\(V\_{nb}\\) = 100 V

এদের মাঝের angle = 60°।

এখন আমরা দুটো vector যোগ করছি। Vector যোগ করলে magnitude সবসময় সাধারণ যোগফলের মতো হয় না। Angle বিবেচনা করতে হয়।

তাই আমরা cosine rule ব্যবহার করেছি।

আর যেহেতু দুইটা vector-এর magnitude সমান, resultant তাদের মধ্যবর্তী 60° angle-কে সমান দুই ভাগ করে।

তাই resultant-এর angle:

\\[ 60^\circ\div2=30^\circ \\]

এজন্য positive sequence-এ line voltage 30° lead করে।

Exam Tip: KVL equation, vector addition formula, √3 result এবং 30° conclusion—এই চারটি অংশ অবশ্যই দেখাবে।

# Question 4 — 6 Marks

A balanced Y-Y system has a phase voltage of 200 V and load impedance of \\(10\angle30^\circ\\;\Omega\\) per phase. Calculate all three phase currents, line currents and total real power. Assume abc sequence.

এটা সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ numerical practice। এখন একদম ধাপে ধাপে করি।

## Step 1: Write Given Data

Given,

Phase voltage:

\\[ V_P=200\\;V \\]

Load impedance:

\\[ Z_P=10\angle30^\circ\\;\Omega \\]

Phase sequence = abc (positive).

We have to determine:

1. Phase currents \\(I_a,I_b,I_c\\)
2. Line currents \\(I_A,I_B,I_C\\)
3. Total real power \\(P\\)

## Step 2: Determine Phase Voltages

For positive sequence:

\\[ V\_{an}=V_P\angle0^\circ \\]

\\[ V\_{bn}=V_P\angle-120^\circ \\]

\\[ V\_{cn}=V_P\angle120^\circ \\]

Since \\(V_P=200\\;V\\),

\\[ \boxed{V\_{an}=200\angle0^\circ\\;V} \\]

\\[ \boxed{V\_{bn}=200\angle-120^\circ\\;V} \\]

\\[ \boxed{V\_{cn}=200\angle120^\circ\\;V} \\]

## Step 3: Calculate Phase Currents

Using Ohm's Law:

\\[ I=\frac{V}{Z} \\]

মনে রাখবে, polar form-এ divide করলে:

\\[ \frac{A\angle\alpha}{B\angle\beta} =\frac AB\angle(\alpha-\beta) \\]

অর্থাৎ magnitude ভাগ এবং angle বিয়োগ।

### Phase a Current

\\[ I_a=\frac{V\_{an}}{Z_P} \\]

\\[ I_a=\frac{200\angle0^\circ}{10\angle30^\circ} \\]

\\[ I_a=\frac{200}{10}\angle(0^\circ-30^\circ) \\]

\\[ \boxed{I_a=20\angle-30^\circ\\;A} \\]

### Phase b Current

\\[ I_b=\frac{V\_{bn}}{Z_P} \\]

\\[ I_b=\frac{200\angle-120^\circ}{10\angle30^\circ} \\]

\\[ I_b=20\angle(-120^\circ-30^\circ) \\]

\\[ \boxed{I_b=20\angle-150^\circ\\;A} \\]

### Phase c Current

\\[ I_c=\frac{V\_{cn}}{Z_P} \\]

\\[ I_c=\frac{200\angle120^\circ}{10\angle30^\circ} \\]

\\[ I_c=20\angle(120^\circ-30^\circ) \\]

\\[ \boxed{I_c=20\angle90^\circ\\;A} \\]

### Phase Current Summary

| Phase | Phase Voltage               | Phase Current              |
| ----- | --------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| a     | \\(200\angle0^\circ\\) V    | \\(20\angle-30^\circ\\) A  |
| b     | \\(200\angle-120^\circ\\) V | \\(20\angle-150^\circ\\) A |
| c     | \\(200\angle120^\circ\\) V  | \\(20\angle90^\circ\\) A   |

বাংলায় বুঝো: প্রত্যেক phase-এর voltage আলাদা angle-এ আছে। কিন্তু impedance তিন phase-এই সমান। তাই current magnitude একই 20 A, শুধু angle আলাদা।

## Step 4: Calculate Line Currents

For a balanced Y-connected load:

\\[ \boxed{I_L=I_P} \\]

Therefore,

\\[ \boxed{I_A=I_a=20\angle-30^\circ\\;A} \\]

\\[ \boxed{I_B=I_b=20\angle-150^\circ\\;A} \\]

\\[ \boxed{I_C=I_c=20\angle90^\circ\\;A} \\]

Hence, the magnitude of each line current is:

\\[ \boxed{I_L=20\\;A} \\]

Y connection-এ line current এবং phase current সমান।

## Step 5: Calculate Total Real Power

For a balanced three-phase system:

\\[ \boxed{P=3V_PI_P\cos\phi} \\]

Here,

\\[ V_P=200\\;V \\]

\\[ I_P=20\\;A \\]

Impedance angle,

\\[ \phi=30^\circ \\]

Therefore,

\\[ P=3\times200\times20\times\cos30^\circ \\]

We know,

\\[ \cos30^\circ=0.8660 \\]

So,

\\[ P=3\times200\times20\times0.8660 \\]

\\[ P=12000\times0.8660 \\]

\\[ P=10392.3\\;W \\]

Therefore,

\\[ \boxed{P=10.39\\;kW} \\]

Since impedance angle is positive, the current lags the voltage.

Thus, power factor:

\\[ \boxed{PF=0.866\text{ lagging}} \\]

# Question 5 — 2 Marks

What is the neutral current in an ideal balanced Y-Y system? Explain briefly.

## Exam-ready Answer

In an ideal balanced Y-Y system, the neutral current is zero because the three-phase currents have equal magnitudes and are displaced by 120° from one another.

By Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL), the neutral current is the phasor sum of three phase currents:

\\[ I_n=I_a+I_b+I_c \\]

For a balanced system:

\\[ I_a+I_b+I_c=0 \\]

Therefore,

\\[ \boxed{I_n=0} \\]

Conclusion: The neutral wire carries no current under ideal balanced loading conditions. However, it carries the resultant unbalanced current when the loads are unequal.

## বাংলায় ব্যাখ্যা

ধরো, তিনটি phase-এর current:

\\[ I_a=10\angle0^\circ A \\]

\\[ I_b=10\angle-120^\circ A \\]

\\[ I_c=10\angle120^\circ A \\]

এদের magnitude সমান হলেও direction আলাদা।

তাই তিনটি current-এর phasor যোগফল:

\\[ I_a+I_b+I_c=0 \\]

অর্থাৎ Neutral Current = 0 A।
